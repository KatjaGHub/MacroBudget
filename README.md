# 🍽️ MacroBudget

A cozy little web app for tracking:

* meals
* macros
* grocery costs
* recipes
* meal planning
* and eventually receipt scanning 👀

Built because I wanted something like MyFitnessPal — but with better cost tracking for real groceries and real meals.

---

## ✨ Features (planned)

### 🥗 Meal Tracking

* breakfast, lunch, dinner & snacks
* custom meals
* recipe logging
* add side dishes separately

### 💪 Macro Tracking

* calories
* protein
* carbs
* fats

### 💸 Cost Tracking

* ingredient prices
* cost per serving
* cost per meal
* weekly food budget overview

### 🧾 Receipt Scanner (future)

* upload grocery receipts
* auto-detect products & prices
* update ingredient costs automatically

### 👥 Shared Household

* use together with your partner
* shared recipes
* shared grocery tracking
* shared meal planning
